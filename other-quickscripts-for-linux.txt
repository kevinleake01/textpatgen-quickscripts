----- OTHER QUICKSCRIPTS FOR LINUX -----

For users using Linux, there are several built-in commands that generate
Text Pattern like files very quickly. Here are some examples, with the
Text Pattern Generated file saved to a filename starting with "00_" :

--- UUIDGEN ---

$ uuidgen -t -C 25 > 00_20260514_114720.txt   # 25x UUID v1 line of text

$ uuidgen -r -C 25 > 00_20260514_114839.txt   # 25x UUID v4 line of text

$ uuidgen -6 -C 25 > 00_20260514_115007.txt   # 25x UUID v6 line of text

$ uuidgen -7 -C 25 > 00_20260514_115113.txt   # 25x UUID v7 line of text

--- OD ---

$ od -t x2 -w32 file.bin > 00_20260514_115630.txt  # Random numbers in Hex, read from file.bin

$ od -t d2 -w20 file.bin > 00_20260514_115832.txt  # Random numbers in Decimal, read from file.bin

--- HEXDUMP ---

$ hexdump -e '15 2 "X-%04X " 1 2 " X-%04X\n"' file.bin > 00_20260514_120320.txt   # Random numbers in Hex, read from file.bin

$ hexdump -e '9 2 "D-%05u " 1 2 " D-%05u\n"' file.bin > 00_20260514_120458.txt   # Random numbers in Decimal, read from file.bin

--- BASENC ---

$ basenc --base16 -w64 file.bin > 00_20260514_121604.txt   # Random numbers in Hex, read from file.bin

$ basenc --base32 -w64 file.bin > 00_20260514_121755.txt   # Random numbers in Base32, read from file.bin

$ basenc --base64 -w64 file.bin > 00_20260514_121936.txt   # Random numbers in Base64, read from file.bin

--- SEQ ---

$ seq -w -s ":" 0 25 > 00_20260514_123719.txt   # Count 0 to 25, separated by colons, all on one line
